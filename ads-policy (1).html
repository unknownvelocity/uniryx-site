Ads Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Uniryx is free to play. Ads help us keep it that way. This policy explains how ads work in the App. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. Rewarded ads only

Uniryx uses rewarded ads. These are ads you choose to watch in return for a reward such as coins or bonus items. We do not force ads on you while you play, and we do not show ads that are disguised as game buttons or content.

2. How rewards work

  • You will always be told what reward you will get before you start watching.
  • The reward is given after you watch the ad fully, as required by the ad provider.
  • If you close the ad early, or the ad fails to load or finish, the reward may not be given. You can try again later.
  • Rewards may have daily limits or waiting times, which are shown in the App.
  • Rewards have no cash value and follow the rules for virtual items in our Terms.

3. Ad providers

Ads are provided by third-party advertising partners such as Google AdMob, AppLovin MAX (and the ad networks connected to it) and Unity Ads. We may use one or more of these, and may add or change partners over time. They may use your advertising ID, device information and IP address to show and measure ads, and may show personalized ads where allowed. Their use of data is covered by their own privacy policies.

4. Your choices

  • Watching ads is optional. You can play without watching any ads.
  • You can turn off ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Making an in-app purchase does not change how rewarded ads work unless we say so in the App.

5. Ad content

We ask our partners to show ads that are suitable for a general audience. We do not want to show ads that are violent, adult, misleading or illegal. If you see an inappropriate ad, please email us with a screenshot (if possible), the time and your device model so we can report it to the ad provider and get it blocked.

6. Fair use of ads

You must not click ads by mistake on purpose, use bots or tools to watch or click ads, or exploit bugs to get rewards. This can lead to loss of rewards, and suspension of your account. Invalid ad activity can also cause ad providers to penalize the App.

7. Ads and children

Uniryx is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly show personalized ads to users we know to be under the applicable age.

8. Changes

We may change how ads work, including the rewards, limits or ad partners. Any updates will be posted here.

Contact us

Questions, requests or complaints? Email us at uniryxsupport@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 7 working days.
