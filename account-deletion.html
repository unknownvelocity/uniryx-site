Account Deletion

Effective date: September 19, 2026

You can ask us to delete your Uniryx account and the data linked to it at any time. Here is how.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to uniryxsupport@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up for Uniryx.
  2. Use the subject Delete my account and include your Uniryx username.
  3. We will confirm that the request is from you, and may reply to ask for a small detail to verify your identity.
  4. We will delete your account and send you a confirmation email, within 30 days of a verified request.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (email address and user ID).
  • Your username and profile information.
  • Your game progress, scores, coins and items.
  • Your global chat messages linked to your account, which are deleted or made anonymous.

What we may keep

  • Purchase and transaction records, for as long as tax, accounting or other laws require.
  • Limited security and anti-fraud logs, kept only as long as needed to protect the App and other players.
Please note: deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You will lose all progress and purchased items, and no refund is given for them. See our Purchase Policy.

Want to delete only some data?

If you only want certain data removed (for example, your chat messages) and want to keep your account, say so in your email and we will review your request.

More on how we handle your data is in our Privacy Policy.
