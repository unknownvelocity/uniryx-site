Purchase Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

This policy explains how in-app purchases work in Uniryx. It is part of our Terms of Service.

1. What you can buy

The in-app shop offers optional virtual items, such as coins, skins and other in-game items, that can be paid for with real money. Everything in Uniryx can also be enjoyed without spending money, and the games are free to play.

2. How payments work

  • All purchases are processed by Google Play using your Google Play payment method.
  • We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Prices are shown in your local currency in the store and include any taxes that apply. The final price is confirmed by Google Play before you pay.

3. Virtual items

  • Virtual coins and items have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn, sold, traded or exchanged for money.
  • Items are licensed to you for use in the App, tied to your account, and can not be transferred to another account.
  • We may change item prices, offers, contents or availability at any time. Changes do not affect items you already own.
  • If the App is discontinued, we will make reasonable efforts to give notice.

4. Delivery

Purchased items are added to your account right after payment is confirmed. If they do not appear, restart the App and check your internet connection. If they are still missing after a few minutes, contact us using the steps in section 6.

5. Refunds

Because virtual items are delivered instantly, purchases are generally final and non-refundable, except where:

  • The law in your country requires a refund.
  • Google Play's refund policy allows it. You can request a refund directly at Google Play, and requests made shortly after purchase are often handled by Google.
  • You were charged but did not receive the item, or you were charged more than once by mistake. In these cases we will fix the issue or help arrange a refund.

If your account is banned for breaking our Terms, purchased items and any unused balance may be forfeited without refund.

6. Payment problems

If you have a failed payment, a double charge, or missing items, email uniryxsupport@gmail.com with:

  • Your username in the App.
  • The Google Play Order ID (found in your Google Play receipt email, starting with GPA.).
  • A short description and, if possible, a screenshot of the issue.

We will investigate and reply within 7 working days.

7. Age requirement

Uniryx and its in-app purchases are for users aged 18 and older only. By making a purchase you confirm that you are 18 or older and are allowed to use the payment method. If we find that a purchase was made by someone under 18, we may review it, cancel it where appropriate, and suspend the account.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.

Contact us

Questions, requests or complaints? Email us at uniryxsupport@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 7 working days.
