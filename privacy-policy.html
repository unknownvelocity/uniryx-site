Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Uniryx ("Uniryx", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you use the Uniryx mobile app (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details you use to sign up through Firebase Authentication, such as your email address (and your name and profile photo if you sign in with Google), plus a unique user ID.
  • Your username or display name and any profile details you choose to add.
  • Messages and content you post in the global chat.
  • Information you send to us when you contact support (for example your email address and message).

Information collected automatically

  • Device information such as device model, operating system version, language and app version.
  • Advertising ID and general ad interaction data (for example whether a rewarded ad was completed).
  • Game progress, scores, in-app currency and items, and how you use features of the App.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address, and crash and performance logs.

Purchase information

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was completed (such as order ID and item purchased) but we do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, run and improve the games, shop and chat.
  • To save your progress and deliver items you purchase or earn.
  • To show rewarded ads and give you the reward after you watch them.
  • To keep the App safe: detecting cheating, fraud, spam and abuse, and moderating the global chat.
  • To fix bugs, measure performance and respond to support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Global chat

The global chat is public. Anything you write there can be seen by all players, and may be stored and reviewed by us for safety. Do not share personal information such as your phone number, address, passwords or financial details in chat. We may remove messages and restrict accounts that break our Terms of Service. You can report a message or player by emailing us.

4. Ads and third-party services

Uniryx shows rewarded ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. To do this we use third-party advertising and app services (such as Google AdMob, AppLovin MAX and the ad networks connected to it, Unity Ads, and Google Play Services). These providers may collect your advertising ID, device information and IP address to deliver and measure ads, and may show ads based on your interests where allowed by law. See our Ads Policy for details.

You can limit ad personalization by resetting or deleting your advertising ID or opting out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. AppLovin's policy is at applovin.com/privacy and Unity's is at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. These third parties have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Firebase. We use Google Firebase (such as Firebase Authentication and cloud database and storage services) to create and sign you in to your account and to store your account data, game progress and chat messages. Firebase may also collect device identifiers and diagnostic data to run these services. Learn more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run the App (hosting, analytics, ads, payments, crash reporting), under obligations to protect it.
  • When required by law, court order or a valid government request.
  • To protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the public or Uniryx.
  • In connection with a merger, sale or transfer of the App, in which case we will ask that this policy continues to be respected.
  • Content you post in the global chat is visible to other players.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide the App and for legal, security and fraud-prevention purposes. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email uniryxsupport@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject "Delete my account" and your username. See our Account Deletion page for details. We will process valid requests within 30 days. Some records (such as purchase records) may be kept longer where the law requires.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Age requirement (18+)

Uniryx is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that a person under 18 has created an account or given us personal information, we will delete the account and the related data. If you believe a person under 18 is using Uniryx, please contact us.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (including under applicable Indian data protection law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. To use these rights, email us at uniryxsupport@gmail.com. You can also uninstall the App at any time to stop all data collection through it.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers in countries other than yours. By using the App you understand that your information may be transferred and processed there.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions, requests or complaints? Email us at uniryxsupport@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 7 working days.
